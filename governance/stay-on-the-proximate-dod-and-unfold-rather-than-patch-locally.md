정수님's standing supervision directive, 2026-08-13, **verbatim**:

> "일을 너무 복잡하게 만들지 말고요. 그리고 야크 쉐이빙 할 경우가 있어요, 워커가. 그럴 때는 야크 쉐이빙 하지 않도록 잘 그 원래의 DoD에 집중해서 잘 진행할 수 있도록 해주시고, 뭔가 국제적인 어떤 모순이나 디시전 메이킹 해야 될 것들이 있을 수 있어요. 그런데 그럴 때는 빅 픽처를 보고 그리고 나온 사안에 대한 어떤 시그니피컨스를 보고 그리고 언폴딩 할 수 있는 시퀀스를 따라서 그렇게 작업을 해야 됩니다. 그렇게 작업이 이루어질 수 있도록 감독을 해주십시오."

⚠️ **"국제적인" is a transcription artifact — almost certainly "국지적인" (local).** Recorded as heard, not silently corrected. Do not quote the word as though its meaning were settled.

**Four rules:**

1. **Don't overcomplicate.** A track does not grow side-quests, extra structure, or scope it was not given.
2. **Yak-shaving watch — the proximate DoD is the leash.** When a worker drifts into fixing something *adjacent* to unblock the "real" work, pull it back to the **original DoD of its track**. [[butler-dod-vs-ultimate-goal]] judges work against the ultimate goal; this adds the other side — **escalate rather than wander**. An adjacent defect found mid-task is recorded and reported, not fixed.
3. **A local contradiction does not get a local reflexive fix.** Look at the **big picture**, weigh the **significance** of the thing that surfaced (deferring is a valid outcome), and follow a **sequence that unfolds** without breaking what already works — then act.
4. **The steward supervises to this standard.** Check-ins verify a worker is **on its original DoD**, not merely that it is busy. "Busy" and "on track" are different observations and only one is the job.

## The resolution: principal-directed displacement ends by finish-out

Applying rule 2 honestly on the night it was issued surfaced that **most of the sprawl was not worker drift — it traced to 정수님's own direct instructions** to those sessions. Raised to him; his answer, **verbatim**:

> "원래 DoD 우선으로 되돌리세요. 직접 지시한 것들은 마무리만 하고요."

So the full shape is:

- **A worker doing what the principal asked is not wandering.** Never label principal-directed work as yak-shaving — it would teach workers to hesitate on his instructions, which is worse than the sprawl.
- While it is happening, the supervisory move is **visibility, not correction**: state plainly that the original DoD has been displaced, so the displacement is a *seen* fact rather than a silent one.
- **Displacement ends by FINISH-OUT.** Directly-instructed work is carried to its **nearest clean completion point** — land what is in flight, close what is open — then stopped. **Not extended, not given new scope, and never dropped mid-flight.** Then the original DoD reclaims priority.
- **Define each track's finish-out list explicitly and say "this is all of it."** Left vague, "finish out" silently readmits the sprawl it is meant to end.

**Precision trap 1 — "original DoD reclaims priority" ≠ "the hold on it is lifted."** One track's DoD was stopped by a *separate standing decision*, not by displacement. Restoring priority does not unblock it, and a worker can easily read it as a green light. Say so explicitly. (Inverse of [[butler-unblocking-restores-the-option-not-its-priority]].)

## Precision trap 2 — "return to your DoD" presumes a DoD remains. Often it does not.

**Hit twice in one night, on 2 of 4 active tracks** (16-scheduler, 472-image-edit). Both had already
satisfied their original DoD days earlier; the night's work was principal-directed side work. The
reprioritization instruction was written as though a displaced DoD were sitting there waiting.

**Why this is dangerous rather than merely inaccurate:** a worker told to *return to its DoD* when
that DoD is already met is under **pressure to manufacture work to satisfy the instruction.** The
instruction supplies a goal; the goal is already met; the compliant-looking move is to invent
something that resembles the DoD. **That is yak-shaving generated by the anti-yak-shaving
directive itself** — which is exactly the failure this principle exists to prevent.

**472-image-edit did it right, and the sequence is the template.** Its DoD read *"design + create
sub-issues"* — an instruction to **create issues** — arriving right as 정수님's issue-creation gate
came down. The two collided head-on. It **read before creating**: queried `sub_issues` on the parent,
found both already existed *and* were already implemented, separated three similarly-named issues
that were **not** sub-issues, **created zero**, and then asked *"it looks like I have nothing left —
is that right?"* rather than filling the silence with work. The steward confirmed by running the same
query independently rather than accepting the report.

**The general shape:** *an instruction to resume presumes there is something to resume.* Verify the
DoD's live state **before** issuing the return, and make **"already satisfied"** an explicitly
welcomed answer — otherwise the worker learns that reporting "nothing to do" reads as failure.

**How to apply:**
- Every check-in names the worker's **original DoD explicitly** and asks for confirmation against it. Do not accept an activity report as evidence of direction.
- **Before telling a worker to return to its DoD, check whether that DoD is still open** — and say in the same message that *"already met, nothing to do"* is a complete and correct answer. When it comes back, **verify it against the artifact yourself** (query the tracker, don't read the summary).
- **A DoD phrased as "create X" must be re-read against any newer gate on creating X.** The DoD is older than the gate; the gate wins, and the correct move is *measure first, create only the remainder* — possibly zero.
- Standing worker instruction: **an adjacent defect is recorded and reported, never fixed on the way past.**
- **A defect the DoD's own gate surfaces, inside the DoD's own code, is IN-DoD — not scope creep.** Fixing it is not yak-shaving; yak-shaving is fixing what is *adjacent*. Test: does the defect defeat the DoD's purpose? A tool that returns an opaque internal error on realistic operator input is the feature not working.
- When a worker proposes expanding scope to resolve something it hit: **narrow it, ask, and let the principal decide** whether the corner cases are in scope this round.
- **Verify the reprioritization landed in each session, not merely that it was sent** ([[butler-verify-delivery]]) — the principal asked for landed, and sent-vs-landed is the distinction that fails silently.
